# CV conventions (sourced notes)

## Indian CV conventions
Source: web search over vendor guides (gptprompts.ai/cv-format-india, resumegeni.com/blog/how-to-write-resume-india, naukri.com/career-advice/standard-resume-format, goodspace.ai, monster.com/career-advice/resume/indian-resume-format). Search snippets only; pages not individually opened. Vendor blogs, treat as conventions.

- Declaration line: sources disagree. Expected in government/PSU/biodata formats (signed; omission can invalidate), often omitted for product companies and international roles, optional for freshers.
- Notice period: commonly stated; recruiters favour immediate to 30 days. Current/expected CTC is a line like "Current CTC | Expected CTC | Notice Period" only when asked; Naukri has a separate Desired CTC field.
- Personal details block (near bottom): date of birth, languages known, current location, notice period. DOB, father's name, marital status are optional but common for traditional/PSU roles; advice is to omit caste/religion.
- Academics: Indian grading varies, so state the scale: "CGPA 8.2/10" or "78%".
- Length: freshers 1-2 pages, experienced 2-3. Naukri favours .docx or text-based PDF over scanned images.

## LLM-written CV tells
Sources: simplify.jobs/blog/can-recruiters-tell-if-ai-wrote-your-resume, ucstrategies.com, scale.jobs/blog/signs-recruiters-know-resume-ai-written, rezi.ai, resume.co/blog/how-to-identify-ai-generated-resumes (search snippets).
- Stacked stock adjectives: "dynamic", "results-driven", "detail-oriented", "expert", "cutting-edge".
- Polished buzzwords with no concrete example; "motivated team player" with no measurable outcome.
- Accomplishments that read like rewritten job descriptions, not quantified results.
- Overall sameness / lack of individual voice. Consumer AI detectors are unreliable.
