# Mid-year review 2026 - HRBP guide

Audience: HRBPs and People Ops coordinators supporting Hyderabad and Pune managers.

## Timeline (2026)
- 1-5 June: managers open "Mid-Year Check-in" business process in Workday; employees self-review by 12 June
- 15-26 June: manager reviews + HRBP spot check of 10% of ratings
- 29 June - 3 July: calibration sessions (see section 3)
- 6 July: letters / outcomes visible

## 1. Before the cycle
1. Pull the "Active workers without manager" report from Workday and fix the supervisory org gaps. Reviews route to the manager of record, so a wrong manager means the review goes to the wrong person.
2. Check transfers in the last 90 days. Workers who moved teams get reviewed by the new manager with input from the old one (use "Request feedback" task).
3. Confirm leave-of-absence cases. Do not send review tasks to workers on LOA > 60 days; handle by exception.

## 2. Common cases
- **New joiners < 90 days:** no rating, check-in note only
- **Manager left mid-cycle:** reassign via "Change Business Process Participant", HRBP initiates, skip-level approves
- **Contractor conversions:** KCC- ID history does not carry reviews. Ask managers to attach prior feedback as a document.
- **Acquired (Ledgerly) staff:** legacy LGR ids may show duplicate worker records. Raise a RITM to People Systems before opening the task.

## 3. Calibration
Distribution is guidance, not a quota. Bring evidence: two project outcomes and one peer input per person proposed for top or bottom bands. HRBP records decisions in the calibration sheet the same day.

## 4. Escalations
Rating disputes go to HRBP first, then Head of People Ops. Timeline for response: 5 working days.
