## Purpose
A summary for the April planning session of what the design team learned in January to March and what we recommend investigating. This is an input to planning, not the plan.

## Studies in the quarter
1. Invoice PO number and footer concept test (5 participants). Outcome: supports adding the fields; one customer needs bespoke layout.
2. Billing settings task review (6 administrators). Outcome: link from billing settings to company profile; tax field move deferred. See the separate page.
3. Support ticket read-through (120 tickets tagged "settings", read by me, themes confirmed with Arun). Outcome: most are location of a setting, not absence of one.

## What I recommend and who decides
- I recommend scheduling a follow-up on bulk retry for the exception queue. Two operators raised it unprompted in 2025 and nobody has tested it.
- I recommend the permissions work lands before any further settings restructuring; the settings study could not test invite flows.
- Whether any of this enters the quarter's roadmap is Meenal's call. She weighs it against the integration commitments that I do not have visibility into. I presented, took questions and updated the open-questions list after the session.

## What the data does not show
All three studies sample customers who agreed to talk to us. They skew to long-tenured accounts and exclude Berlin. The ticket read-through covers only tickets tagged "settings", so contacts filed under other tags are missing.
