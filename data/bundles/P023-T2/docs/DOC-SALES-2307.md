Use this deck skeleton for QBR. Pull numbers from the account dashboard the day before, not the morning of.

Slides: 1) Where we started (goals from kickoff) 2) Match rate trend, last 4 quarters 3) Exceptions: top 3 reasons and age 4) Support tickets in the quarter (Neelam pulls list from INC) 5) What we plan next quarter 6) Renewal date and anything commercial - say it, don't hide it.

Talk track tips: start with their goal not our features. If match rate fell, say it first. If a ticket was our fault, say sorry once and show the RCA.

Do not promise roadmap dates in QBR. Say "planned" and check with PM.
