Fill this in BEFORE the sandbox tenant is created. If the customer will not agree criteria, the POC is a demo and we do not call it a POC.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Duration | 30 or 60 days max, end date in writing |
| Data in scope | 2 months of payout reports, 1 bank feed |
| Success metric | >= 85% auto-match, exception queue < 50 open at day 30 |
| Customer owners | Finance owner AND developer named |
| Kestrel owner | SE (me), AE for commercial |
| Exit | Signed one-pager of results. Win = proceed to paper. Lose = reason code in Salesforce |

AE tip from Dana: put the pricing call date in the template, otherwise POCs drift.
