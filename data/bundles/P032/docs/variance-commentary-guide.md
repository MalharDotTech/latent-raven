## Audience
The monthly pack goes to department heads and the CFO. Most readers look at the first page and the commentary on lines that moved by more than 5 percent or more than Rs 10 lakh. Commentary should let a reader decide without opening the workbook.

## Structure of one comment
1. What moved: "Cloud hosting is 18 percent over forecast for September."
2. Why: one cause, with a number. "Two customers moved to the EU region earlier than planned, adding Rs 14 lakh."
3. Whether it recurs: one-off, timing, or run-rate.
4. What we are doing or assuming in the forecast.

## Do and don't
- Do separate timing from permanent differences. A vendor invoice booked a month late is timing.
- Do name the owner of the explanation ("per Platform"), so the reader knows who to ask.
- Don't write "due to various factors". If we do not know, write "under review with <owner>, update next month".
- Don't paste a table into a comment; link the tab.

## Example (edited by Lavanya)
Before: "Travel expense higher due to events."
After: "Travel is Rs 6.2 lakh over forecast (31 percent), all from the October customer summit booked in September; timing against the Q4 forecast, no change to full-year."

## Review
I draft, Lavanya edits for tone and anything the CFO is likely to ask about. Over the last four quarters the number of her edits per pack went from about 14 to 5, and she asks me to add the "recurs?" line when I forget it.
