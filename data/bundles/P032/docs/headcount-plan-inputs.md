## Purpose
Finance needs hires, start dates and exits from every department each quarter to build the salary line. This page describes how inputs are requested, what we do when they do not arrive, and how HR and Finance numbers are reconciled.

## Timeline
- Working day 1: I send the input sheet to department owners (about 22 owners). One row per role, columns for status (approved, open, backfill), expected start date, and band.
- Working day 5: deadline. By day 3 I send a reminder to anyone who has not opened the sheet; by day 5 I call the five or six who are still missing.
- Working day 6-7: I compare the sheet with Shilpa's HR report of approved requisitions and joiner/leaver list. Differences are listed on the reconciliation tab and I message the owner with the specific role and date in question, not a general query.
- Working day 8: Lavanya reviews.

## Common issues
- Start dates entered as "Q3". We convert to the first working day of the mid-quarter month and flag the row, then ask for a date.
- Backfills counted as new hires, double counting the salary for the overlap. I mark overlap months explicitly.
- Hiring freeze notices that Finance receives later than the departments do. Shilpa now forwards them the same day.
- Two owners claiming the same requisition after a reorganisation. I ask both and wait for HR to confirm the cost centre.

## What the numbers are used for
The output feeds the salary and benefits lines of the cost forecast workbook (see DATA space). Lavanya presents the movement to the CFO. Decisions about whether a role is approved sit with department heads and HR, not with FP&A; my job is to make the cost visible and flag when the sheet and the HR report disagree.

## Tips for new analysts
Keep the email trail in the same folder as the sheet. When an owner says "it was in the earlier version", you need to show the date you received what.
